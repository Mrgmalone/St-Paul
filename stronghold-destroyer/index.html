HOW TO PLAY

Select: left-click a person, or drag a box around several. Double-click selects everyone on screen. On touch, tap or drag.

Command: right-click (or tap) the ground to move, an enemy to attack, a tree for wood, a berry bush or farm for food.

View: push the mouse against any edge of the screen (or use the arrow keys) to scroll; mouse wheel or pinch to zoom; click the map in the corner to jump. Turn and tilt (3D): the Turn and Tilt buttons at the bottom left, Q and E to turn, R and F to tilt, a two-finger sideways swipe, or hold Option (Alt) and drag. Space or the Castle button brings you home.

Command panel (right side): click Town, Castle, Army, Rule or Industry to drop that list down (keys 1 to 5). Scroll the panel with the mouse wheel. Hide (or the Tab key) folds it away.

Workers: a Woodcutter's Hut or a Farm takes an idle civilian as its worker by itself. Stockpiles and Granaries are closer places to carry wood and food. People with no job wait at the campfire. A direct order takes a civilian off their job.

Industry: hop farms, breweries and taverns make ale, which lifts mood. Iron mines sit beside rusty iron rocks. Bowyers, spear-makers and smithies make weapons for the armory; every soldier needs a weapon from the armory plus 10 gold to train. Make weapons in the workshops over time, or buy them in the Army tab once you have an armory (sword 40 gold, spear 20, bow 30). You start with 2 spears and 2 bows.

Popularity: click Mood for every factor. Two or three kinds of food in store, ale, chapels and gardens raise mood; high taxes, crowding and stocks lower it. Stocks make people work harder; gardens make them work slower.

Build: pick a building, then click near your keep. Walls: click the start, then the end. Right-click or Esc cancels.

Mood: click Mood at the top to see what is raising or lowering it. Happy people work faster and settlers arrive; unhappy people leave.

Stone castles: a Quarry beside a grey rock cuts stone. High Walls stand as tall as your keep; archers climb them by Stairs placed beside the wall (or from a tower joined to it). Stone Towers and the Great Tower are far tougher than the wooden Tower, shoot farther, and hold more archers.

Outlines: when a wall, tower or building hides your people (or enemies), you see their shape through it in their team colour. Turn this off in the Menu.

Training: click a soldier or civilian button again to queue more, up to 10 of each. The green ring empties as each one is made; the number shows how many are waiting; right-click a button to cancel one. If you cannot train, the button says why.

Rally points: in the Army list, choose Army rally point (new soldiers) or Town rally point (new civilians and rams), then click the ground. A flag marks each one. Send army to rally point gathers all your soldiers there.

The enemy: its workers cut wood and gather food and carry them home, and with them it builds up its camp: houses, towers and walls. Killing its workers slows it down. Raids come regularly, along the roads and through the woods from the north, the south and the west; a red ring on the map shows where. Difficulty (chosen at the start) sets how fast the enemy works and how often and how hard it raids.

Archers on walls: select archers and right-click one of your walls or towers. Up there they shoot farther, take half damage from arrows, and swords cannot reach them. Order them elsewhere to climb down.

Siege: stone walls resist swords and arrows. Battering rams smash walls and buildings but cannot hurt soldiers.

3D view: Q and E turn the camera. V (or the Menu) switches between the 3D and the flat view.

Keys: S stop · Esc clear · H help · M sound on or off.
